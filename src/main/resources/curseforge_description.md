# Jomlua Ender – Technology from the End

**Jomlua Ender** brings a new tier of technology to Minecraft: unbreakable gear made from **Endarium**, armor you can upgrade piece by piece, energy-powered machines and a **multiblock nuclear reactor** that grows with your ambitions.

> ⚠️ **Alpha** – the mod is in active development. Please back up your worlds.

---

## ⛏️ New Materials
- **Endarium** – a rare, energy-charged metal from the End and the heart of this mod
- **Germanium** – found across the Overworld, Nether and End, the backbone of every machine
- **Galaxis Quartz** – grows on cosmic crystal clusters
- **Uranium** – buried deep in the deepslate layer. *Careful: holding it makes you radioactive!*

All ores and ingots use common tags, so processing machines from other mods can handle them too.

## ⚔️ Endarium Gear
The tier **above Netherite**:
- **Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel and Hoe** – faster than Netherite and completely **unbreakable**
- **Endarium Sword** – a devastating blade with **50 attack damage**
- Fire resistant, enchantable and built to last forever

## 🛡️ Upgradeable Armor
Endarium armor is stronger than Netherite – and it grows with you. Every piece can be upgraded with special abilities:

| Piece | Abilities |
|---|---|
| **Helmet** | Night Vision, Water Breathing |
| **Chestplate** | Creative Flight |
| **Leggings** | Fire Immunity, Speed (stackable) |
| **Boots** | No Fall Damage, Jump Boost (stackable) |
| **Every piece** | Extra Health – up to **+10 hearts per piece** |

Installed upgrades are shown right in the tooltip.

## ⚙️ Machines
- **Coal Generator** – turns coal into energy (FE)
- **Electric Smelter** – smelts everything a furnace can, powered by FE
- **Arc Fusion Chamber** – fuses two materials into something new, with visible items and electric arcs inside the glass
- **Germanium Miner** – a multiblock miner that digs for you
- **Speed & Efficiency Upgrades** – stack them to make your machines faster or cheaper to run
- **Side Configuration** – choose input and output for every side; outputs push automatically into pipes, cables and AE2

## ☢️ Nuclear Reactor
The centerpiece of Jomlua Ender: a **multiblock reactor** you can build in any size from **3×3×3 up to 7×7×7**.

- Burns **Uranium** and produces huge amounts of energy – up to **12,500 FE/t**
- The bigger the inside, the more power – and the more heat
- Keep it cool with a steady water supply, or it will shut down at **1000 °C**
- Produces **Nuclear Waste** that has to be dealt with
- **Reactor Ports** switch between Input, Energy and Waste output
- Once complete, the whole structure **merges into one seamless machine**
- Look through **Reactor Glass** and watch the cooling water rise inside

## 💧 Utility
- **Infinite Water Source** – endless water on every side with no transfer limit, works with pipes from any mod
- **Reactor Glass** – connected glass that also makes beautiful windows for your base

## 🔌 Compatibility
- **JEI** – recipe pages for all machines, armor upgrades, mob drops and a reactor building guide
- **Mekanism, EnderIO, AE2** and other tech mods – items, fluids and energy connect through the NeoForge Transfer API
- **Common tags** for ores, ingots, dusts and storage blocks
